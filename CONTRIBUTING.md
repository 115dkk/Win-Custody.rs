# Contributing

## When a pull request waits on you

If the maintainer requests changes on your pull request, or leaves a comment
on it that needs your reply, they may send you a reminder once at least a week
has passed.

The deadline in a reminder is always one week after the reminder is posted.
If by then you have neither replied nor pushed changes, the maintainer may
add their own commits on top of yours and merge the pull request, or close it
if they decide not to take the change. When they add commits, yours stay as
they are, so your authorship remains in the history.

The first wait is at least a week, not exactly a week, because a busy
maintainer may leave a pull request longer before sending a reminder. It is
best to address a review before a reminder arrives.
